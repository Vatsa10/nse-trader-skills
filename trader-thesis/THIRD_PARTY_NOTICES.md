## ADHD (parallel divergent ideation)

trader-thesis adapts the method of the ADHD skill: a pre-flight gate, a diverge
phase of isolated parallel branches under different cognitive frames with
evaluation forbidden, and a converge phase where a critic scores, clusters,
flags traps and deepens the top three. Source: https://github.com/UditAkhourii/adhd

What was adapted: the gate, the two-phase loop and its isolation rule, the
branch and critic instructions, and the idea of a frame table. What is new
here: the trading frames, the shared evidence brief (`thesis.py brief`), the
scoring axes (evidence, materiality, probability), the trading output shape
and its enforcement in code (`thesis.py render`). No ADHD source code is
copied.
